October 29: Halloween's Final Countdown Meets a Byeongja Day's Ravine Water - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck

Published October 29, 2026

October 29 sits two days out from Halloween, now one of Korea's biggest unofficial festival nights - university districts and themed cafes already decorated, costumes planned for the weekend ahead. On the Saju calendar, today is a Byeongja (丙子) day: Yang Fire sitting directly over the Rat's Water branch, fire and water stacked in the same two characters. Its old 'napeum' nickname is 간하수 (gan-ha-su), Ravine Water - the quiet stream that runs beneath a cliff, hidden from the trail above, slowly cutting its own path through solid rock without anyone watching. It's a fitting image for a weekend built around disguises: something moving underneath the surface, unnoticed, until it finally breaks through. Three zodiac animals complete today's Monkey-Rat-Dragon Water Trine with that hidden current running through them, and they're the ones most likely to feel it rising first.

1. Rat - Standing Right Over the Source

It's the Rat's own branch under today's sky, so the ravine's water starts directly beneath your feet. Something you've kept quiet for a while - a plan, a feeling, a side project - has more momentum than you've let on. Today's a good day to stop treating it like a secret and let it surface on its own terms, before someone else notices it first.

2. Dragon - Where the Stream Cuts Through

The Dragon holds the far point of the trine, the spot where patient water finally wears a path through stone. Whatever you've been chipping away at - a goal, a negotiation, a relationship that's been slow to move - shows real progress today, even if it doesn't look dramatic yet. Keep carving. The rock is thinner than it feels.

3. Monkey - Follows the Water Downhill

The Monkey closes the loop, the point where the ravine's current finally reaches open ground. News, money, or an opportunity that's been working its way toward you through indirect channels is close to arriving - possibly through someone you didn't expect to be the messenger. Stay easy to reach today instead of chasing it yourself.
