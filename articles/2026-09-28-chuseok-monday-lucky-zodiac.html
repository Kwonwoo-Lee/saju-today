September 28: The 3 Zodiac Animals Bouncing Back Best After Chuseok

Published September 28, 2026

It's Monday, September 28 — the first day back to normal life after Korea's long Chuseok holiday weekend. Plenty of people are back at their desks with their minds still somewhere on holiday, but a few zodiac animals are finding their rhythm unusually fast today. Here are the three with the best energy right now.

1. Rat — Holiday Planning Turns Into Action

The Rat spent the holiday quietly sorting out plans in their head, and today's the day to start acting on them one by one. Clearing out the backlog of emails and meetings on your first day back may turn up a surprisingly good lead or piece of information. Nail down your priorities in the morning and the rest of the day flows smoothly.

2. Dragon — Holiday Greetings Turn Into Connections

For the Dragon, the check-ins and greetings exchanged over Chuseok keep paying off longer than expected. A conversation with a relative or old contact you hadn't spoken to in a while could turn into real, practical help soon. If someone crosses your mind today, sending a quick message first is worth it.

3. Goat — Fastest to Bounce Back

The Goat tends to shake off holiday fatigue faster than most, so both body and mind are already back on track today. It's a good moment to pick back up routines you let slide — chores, exercise, eating on schedule — and the pace you set now holds steady through the rest of the week.

Still Feeling the Holiday Fog?

No need to worry if you're not one of these three. Feeling off after a long holiday is completely normal, and most people find their rhythm again by midweek. Today's a better day for easing back in gently than for pushing big decisions.
