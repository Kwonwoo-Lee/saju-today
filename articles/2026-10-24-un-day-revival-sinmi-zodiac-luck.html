October 24: UN Day's Revival Campaign Meets a Sinmi Metal-Goat Day - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck

Published October 24, 2026

October 24 marks United Nations Day, the anniversary of the UN Charter taking effect in 1945. In Korea it has its own forgotten history: a national holiday here until 1975, dropped the following year, and in 2026 a renewed signature campaign is pushing to bring it back onto the calendar. On the Saju side, today falls on Sinmi (辛未), a Yin Metal day sitting on the Goat earth branch - metal that's already been refined rather than raw, resting on ground that quietly feeds it. That's a day for finishing what's already polished rather than starting something rough. Three zodiac animals catch this current especially well.

1. Rooster - Metal Finds Its Edge

The Rooster shares today's metal element outright, and a metal-on-metal day sharpens whatever's already close to done - a report, a negotiation, a decision you've been circling. Small edits land better than big overhauls today. If something feels 90% finished, today's the day to call it finished.

2. Goat - Standing on Home Ground

Today's earth branch is the Goat's own sign, so the Goat gets an unusual boost of quiet stability - less pressure to perform, more room to just function well. Conversations that felt stuck for weeks may finally move, simply because you're less tense walking into them. Don't force progress; let it come to you today.

3. Monkey - Outreach Gets Returned

With UN Day's theme of international cooperation in the air, the Monkey's networking instincts pay off fastest - an old contact, a message sent out of nowhere, a favor returned without being asked. If you've been meaning to reconnect with someone across a distance, today's the day that message actually gets answered.
