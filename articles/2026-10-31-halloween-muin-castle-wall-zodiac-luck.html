October 31: Halloween Itself Meets a Muin Day's Castle-Top Earth - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck

Published October 31, 2026

October 31 is Halloween itself - not the eve, not the countdown, but the night everyone's costume and candy bowl has been building toward. On the Saju calendar, today is a Muin (戊寅) day: Yang Earth sitting directly on the Tiger's Wood branch, a stem and branch pair the old calendar nicknamed 성두토 (seongdu-to), Castle-Top Earth. It's the raised earthen wall built along the top of a fortress wall, piled there for one purpose - to hold a line once it gets dark and nobody can say for certain what's moving outside it. Few napeum names fit their night this well. Three zodiac animals complete the Tiger's Horse-Dog Fire Trine today, and they're the ones standing closest to that wall when the sun goes down.

1. Tiger - The Wall's Own Foundation Stone

It's the Tiger's own branch holding up today's sky, so the castle wall is built on ground you already own. Something you've been quietly reinforcing - a boundary, a budget, a decision you made and haven't restated out loud - gets tested today, lightly, almost like someone just checking if it holds. It does. Stand on it without over-explaining yourself.

2. Horse - Where the Rampart Catches the Firelight

The Horse holds the trine's brightest point, the stretch of wall lit up first when the torches go along it. Whatever you've built quietly is about to be seen - by a boss, a crowd, someone deciding who gets credit. Let it be looked at today instead of downplaying it; the wall was built to be visible from exactly this angle.

3. Dog - Posted at the Gate After Dark

The Dog closes the trine at the gatehouse, the one spot in any wall where something is actually let in or turned away. An offer, a message, or a person showing up uninvited tonight needs a real decision, not a reflex one. Trust the gut check you'd normally second-guess - tonight it's unusually accurate.
