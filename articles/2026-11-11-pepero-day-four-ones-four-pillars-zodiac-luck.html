November 11: Pepero Day's Four 1's Echo Your Saju's Four Pillars — the 3 Zodiac Signs Luckiest Today

Published November 11, 2026

Wednesday, November 11 is Pepero Day in Korea — the date everyone swaps boxes of Pepero, the long thin chocolate-covered biscuit stick, because 11/11 looks exactly like four of them standing in a row. It started as informal gift-swapping among students in the 1990s and has since grown into one of the biggest "day marketing" events of the Korean calendar, with roughly half the snack's annual sales landing in the week around today. But step back from the marketing for a second and the date has a second, quieter echo: four matching digits in a line is also a pretty good visual for the four pillars — year, month, day, and hour — that make up a real Saju (BaZi) chart. In the saju calendar, today falls on a Gichuk (己丑) day: Yin Earth sitting on Yin Earth, the sign of the Ox, doubled and stacked rather than pulling in different directions. That kind of alignment, where everything lines up instead of fighting itself, is lighting up three zodiac animals in particular today.

1. Rat — The Direct Bond Pays Off

The Rat shares a six-harmony bond with today's Ox energy, the same kind of easy, locking-in connection that made the Rat first in the zodiac order to begin with. Today that shows up as a plan, a message, or a small gift exchange — Pepero included — landing better than expected. If you've been putting off reaching out to someone, today's the day it goes smoothly instead of awkwardly.

2. Snake — Steady Groundwork Starts Paying Interest

The Snake shares today's metal trine with the Ox, and that means groundwork laid quietly over the past few weeks starts showing a return today, even if the Snake wasn't expecting results yet. A task finished early or a favor done without being asked turns out to matter more than it seemed to at the time. Trust the slow build rather than rushing to force a faster one.

3. Rooster — Clarity Replaces Second-Guessing

The Rooster also shares today's metal trine, and the result is a sharper, more decisive mood than usual — a choice you've been circling becomes obvious rather than overwhelming. Numbers, schedules, or anything that needs precision go smoothly today. Make the call you've been sitting on; today's clarity doesn't usually stick around for long.

Not One of Today's Three? Pepero Day Is for Everyone

A Gichuk day isn't printed on any calendar, and the steady, aligned feeling behind it isn't reserved for just these three signs. If today already feels unusually tidy or well-timed, that's the four-pillar alignment doing its quiet work — no Pepero box required, though it doesn't hurt.
