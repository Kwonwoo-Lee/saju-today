November 25: The Beaver Supermoon's Afterglow Opens a Rare Streak of Three - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck Today

Published November 25, 2026

Last night at 11:53 p.m. Korea time, the Beaver Supermoon reached its exact full phase - and it's still hanging bright and round in tonight's sky on Wednesday, November 25. This isn't just one moon doing its thing: it's the opening act of a rare run of three supermoons in a row, with the Christmas Eve Supermoon due on December 24 and the Wolf Moon supermoon following in January. There's a quiet overlap worth noticing too - today is also a Gyemyo day, a Yin Water stem sitting on the Rabbit branch, so the day's own element echoes the Moon's. In Saju, the Moon's energy maps onto Water, and the three zodiac animals that share the Water trio - Pig, Rat, and Ox - are the ones feeling tonight's afterglow most directly. Here's how each one catches it.

1. Pig - The Spark That Started This Streak

The Pig sits at the opening point of the Water trio, the branch where this kind of energy first gathers before anything else follows. That's a fitting seat for the Pig today, since this Supermoon is itself the opening point of a three-moon streak that won't fully play out until January. For the Pig, today favors starting something whose payoff arrives later rather than now - booking the trip, sending the first message, planting a seed you won't see grow for weeks. What begins tonight has real staying power.

2. Rat - Standing Exactly Where the Moon Peaks

The Rat holds the peak position of the same trio - the point where Water energy runs fullest, between the Pig's opening and the Ox's storage. Tonight, that peak lines up almost exactly with the Moon's own: full, bright, and at its biggest reach of the month. For the Rat, this is a day to let things run at full intensity rather than hold back - put the real pitch in front of someone, say the full version of what you mean, show up at full size. Whatever you bring tonight carries extra weight.

3. Ox - Quietly Locking In What the Moon Started

The Ox sits at the storage point of the trio, where the Water that opened with the Pig and peaked with the Rat finally gets gathered in and kept rather than spent. That's useful today, with a three-supermoon streak only just getting started - someone needs to be the one making sure tonight's momentum doesn't just fade out a month later. For the Ox, today favors locking things down: confirm a plan, put money or effort somewhere you intend to leave it, say yes to the long version of something rather than the quick one.
