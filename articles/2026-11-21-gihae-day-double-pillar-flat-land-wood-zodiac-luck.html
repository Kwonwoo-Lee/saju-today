November 21: A Double Gihae Day Closes Out 'Flat Land Wood' and Hides a Secret Combination Inside Itself - D-1 to Minor Snow - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck Today

Published November 21, 2026

Saturday, November 21 is a Gihae (己亥) day - and this November happens to be a Gihae (己亥) month, so today's day pillar and the month pillar overhead are the exact same stem and branch. That kind of match only lines up once every sixty days, and it tends to amplify whatever the pillar itself is already doing rather than introduce anything new. In the sixty-day nayin table, today also turns the page for real: yesterday's Musul day opened Pyeongji-mok (平地木), Flat Land Wood, and today's Gihae day closes it out, completing the pair. And there's a quieter detail inside the day pillar itself - Yin Earth (己) sits on the Pig branch (亥), whose dominant hidden energy is Water, but buried underneath that is a hidden Wood stem that silently combines with the Earth sitting above it. Nothing about that combination shows on the surface; Korean Saju readers call a pairing like this an 암합, a hidden combination. On top of all that, it's D-1 to Soseol (Minor Snow), the solar term landing Sunday at 4:23pm. Here are the three zodiac animals reading today's doubled, quietly combining energy best.

1. Pig - Today Is Your Day, Doubled, With a Secret Folded Inside It

When the day's own branch is your sign, the day already mirrors you - and today that mirror is doubled, since the month above shares the identical pillar. Pig is also where the Pig-Rabbit-Goat Wood trine quietly begins, its first spark hidden the same way today's inner combination is hidden: present, but not yet visible to anyone else. For Pig, that makes today good for starting something real under the surface - an application, a draft, a quiet first conversation - even if there's nothing to show for it yet by evening.

2. Rabbit - Where That Same Trine Comes Into Full Bloom

Rabbit sits at the flourishing peak of the same Pig-Rabbit-Goat Wood trine that today's hidden combination is tied to, the point where that trine's energy is at its strongest and most visible. For Rabbit, today rewards finishing and presenting rather than starting quietly - a pitch, a piece of writing, a plan that's been sitting half-done is more likely to land well today than it was earlier in the week.

3. Goat - The Trine's Storage Point, Right as the Table Closes a Chapter

Goat is the storage point of that same trine, where its energy finally gets banked rather than spent - a fitting match for a day whose own nayin pairing is closing out, not opening one. For Goat, today favors wrapping things up and putting them safely away: closing out a task list, settling a account, filing something that's been open too long.
