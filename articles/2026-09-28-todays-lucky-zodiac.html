September 28: The 3 Zodiac Signs With the Best Luck Today

Published September 28, 2026

The Sun has settled fully into Libra this week, tilting the general mood toward balance and relationships. If you've been pushing something through on your own, this is a good stretch to bring someone else back into the conversation — and a good week to finally say the thing you've been putting off. A few signs are getting an extra boost right now.

1. Libra — Your Own Season, Your Own Pace

Libra (Sep 23 – Oct 22) is smack in the middle of its solar season. It's an easy day to voice an opinion you've been sitting on, and negotiations or compromises land more persuasively than usual. Watch the flip side, though — your usual habit of putting off decisions is more pronounced today. Pick one choice you've been circling and actually close it out.

2. Leo — Presence on the Stage

Leo catches a favorable angle from Libra today, and self-expression comes easily. Presentations, interviews, anything that puts you in front of people tends to land better than usual. Leave a little more room to actually hear what others think, and today's momentum carries cleanly into next week.

3. Pisces — Instincts Are Unusually Sharp

Pisces gets a clarity boost to both emotion and intuition today. If a relationship or an offer has felt hard to read, trust your first impression. For anything important — a reply, a decision — give it one more check once things settle down in the evening before you send it.
