November 22: Minor Snow Arrives as a Gyeongja Day Opens 'Wall Top Earth' - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck Today

Published November 22, 2026

Sunday, November 22 is Soseol (小雪), Minor Snow - the twentieth of the year's twenty-four solar terms, landing this afternoon at 4:23pm. It isn't the heavy snow of Daeseol (Major Snow), still three weeks off; it's the quieter turn where Korea's cold stops being a mild warning and starts meaning it. Today is also a Gyeongja (庚子) day, Yang Metal sitting on the Rat branch - and because the last Gyeongja day fell exactly sixty days ago, today opens a brand-new pair in the nayin table: Byeoksangto (壁上土), Wall Top Earth, the plaster that means nothing loose or wet, only once it's been set against a wall and allowed to harden. There's a second layer to it, too - the Rat branch under today's pillar happens to be the exact flourishing peak of the Monkey-Rat-Dragon Water trine, one of saju's three great samhap groupings, where Monkey quietly starts the current and Dragon eventually banks it. Here are the three zodiac animals reading today's arriving cold and peaking water energy best.

1. Rat - Today Mirrors You Twice Over

When the day's own branch matches your sign, the day already reflects you - and today that reflection doubles, because the same Rat branch is also the single strongest point of the Monkey-Rat-Dragon Water trine, the spot where that current runs at full flood rather than just starting or settling. For Rat, that makes today good for anything that needs real momentum behind it right now - a decision you've been circling, a conversation you keep postponing, a push that needs to land today rather than drift into next week.

2. Monkey - Where This Same Current First Starts Moving

Monkey sits at the generation point of the same trine, the place where the Water current first stirs before it reaches Rat's full flood. For Monkey, today favors starting rather than finishing - opening a new file, sending the first message, setting something in motion that won't show real results yet, the way today's new nayin pair has only just opened and hasn't hardened into anything yet either.

3. Dragon - The Trine's Storage Point, on a Day That Just Opened an Earth Pair

Dragon is the storage point of the same trine, where the Water current finally gets banked rather than spent - and Dragon is itself an Earth sign, which makes it a natural container for water to settle into, much like wet plaster settling onto a wall before it sets. For Dragon, today favors anything that benefits from being locked in early, while the new nayin pair is still fresh - saving, committing, putting something safely out of reach of second-guessing.
