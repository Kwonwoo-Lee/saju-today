October 28: Correction Day Meets an Eulhae Day's Mountain-Peak Beacon Fire - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck

Published October 28, 2026

October 28 is the 81st 교정의 날 (Gyojeong-ui Nal), Korea's Correction Day - marking the anniversary of 1945, when Korea took its prison and rehabilitation system back from Japanese colonial administration and began running it as its own. It's not a public holiday, mostly observed with quiet award ceremonies honoring correctional officers for work that rarely makes headlines: steering people back onto a steadier path. On the Saju calendar, today is also an Eulhae (乙亥) day - Yin Wood resting over the Pig's Water branch. Its old 'napeum' nickname is 산두화, Mountain-Peak Fire, and it's the very same name carried by yesterday's Gapsul day. Where yesterday's fire stayed buried underground, today it climbs to the summit - the exact spot where Korea's historic 봉수 beacon towers once stood, stone platforms on mountain peaks that relayed urgent news from the border to the king in Seoul, fire by night and smoke by day. A single calm fire on a clear night simply meant 'all is well,' a fitting signal for a day built around quiet correction rather than alarm. Three zodiac animals complete today's Pig-Rabbit-Goat Wood Trine with that branch underfoot, and they're the ones most likely to catch the signal first.

1. Pig - Lights the First Fire

It's the Pig's own branch under today's sky, so whatever signal goes up starts closest to home. Something personal you've been handling quietly on your own - a decision, a fix, a small course correction - is ready to actually go live today. Light it yourself instead of waiting for someone else to strike the match.

2. Rabbit - Carries the Signal Onward

The Rabbit holds the trine's middle point, the relay station that keeps a signal moving instead of dying out on one peak. News or momentum you pass along today travels further than you'd expect - a message, an introduction, an update you've been sitting on is worth sending now. Don't let it stall with you; today it's built to travel.

3. Goat - Where the Message Lands

The Goat closes out the trine, the point where a relayed signal finally reaches somewhere that can act on it. A correction you've been putting off - admitting a mistake, resetting a plan, changing course on something that clearly isn't working - lands better today than it would most days. Let today be the day it actually sticks, not just the day you think about it.
