November 28: A Byeongo Day Blazes With Double Fire, Yet Opens 'Sky River Water' Underneath - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck Today

Published November 28, 2026

Saturday, November 28 is a Byeongo (丙午) day in the sixty-day cycle - Yang Fire sitting directly on the Horse's own branch, and Horse is itself a Fire branch underneath. That makes today about as hot as the calendar gets, fire sitting on fire with nothing in between to soften it. Yet in the nayin table, this exact pairing opens Cheonhasu (天河水), Sky River Water - the old idea that fire pushed to its absolute peak doesn't just keep burning, it turns to rising steam and eventually falls back as water, the same way a kettle left on too long fills a room with mist rather than more flame. Tomorrow's Jeongmi day will close out this same Water pairing. There's a fitting backdrop to it this weekend, too: central Korea is right in the middle of kimjang (김장) season this year, the yearly ritual of salting whole heads of napa cabbage and packing them with fiery red chili paste before burying them in brine to ferment through the winter - literally working fire and water together in the kitchen to make something that lasts months. Here are the three zodiac animals reading today's fire-turning-to-water energy best.

1. Horse - Standing Right in Today's Double Blaze

When the day's own branch matches your sign, the day already speaks your language, and today it's speaking at full volume - Yang Fire sitting on the Horse's own Fire, about as strong as this pairing ever gets. For Horse, that favors anything that benefits from heat and visibility right now: presenting instead of drafting, calling instead of emailing, putting the real version of something in front of someone rather than a softened one. Just keep half an eye on what the nayin underneath is already doing - today's intensity is built to cool into something steadier, not stay this hot forever.

2. Goat - The Partner Who Finishes What Today Opens

Horse and Goat form a direct six-harmony bond on the branch wheel, a closer tie than the trine groupings covered here before, and tomorrow's Jeongmi day belongs to Goat - closing out the very Sky River Water pairing that Byeongo opens today. For Goat, today is less about today itself than about setting up tomorrow: prepping, salting, lining pieces up the way a kimjang cook salts cabbage today so it's ready to pack with chili paste the next day. Whatever starts moving around Goat right now is built to land cleanly within a day.

3. Rat - Built to Read the Water Hiding Under the Fire

Rat sits directly across the branch wheel from Horse, the position every Fire day leans on hardest - but Rat is also a pure Water sign itself, which makes it the animal best placed to sense exactly where today's nayin is already headed. For Rat, the heat and noise everyone else is reacting to today isn't really the story; trusting a quieter, steadier read instead of getting pulled into the loudest version of events tends to pay off, since Rat can see where this particular fire actually ends up.
