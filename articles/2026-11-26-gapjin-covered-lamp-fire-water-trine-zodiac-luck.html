November 26: A Gapjin Day Opens 'Covered Lamp Fire' at the Water Trine's Storage Point - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck Today

Published November 26, 2026

Today, Thursday, November 26, is a Gapjin day on the sexagenary calendar - Yang Wood resting on the Dragon branch. Its nayin name is Bokdeunghwa (覆燈火), literally "Covered Lamp Fire": not a bonfire or a furnace, but the small, steady flame that survives precisely because something is shielding it from the wind. That image fits the week outside your window too, with sunset creeping earlier by the day and the easiest flames to keep burning right now being the sheltered kind. There's a tidier connection underneath the metaphor as well. Dragon is the storage branch of the Water trine it forms with Monkey and Rat - the point where whatever that trine built finally settles, closes, and holds rather than moves. Here's how each of the three catches today's pull.

1. Monkey - Where This Water Trine First Gathers

Monkey sits at the opening point of the trine, the branch where this particular current of Water energy first starts pooling before Rat or Dragon ever touch it. For Monkey, that makes today a day for starting rather than finishing - raising an idea nobody's heard yet, reaching out to someone new, putting the first small amount of effort into something whose real shape won't show for weeks. What begins today has a trine's worth of follow-through waiting behind it.

2. Rat - Standing at the Trine's Fullest Point

Rat holds the peak of the same three-branch current, the point between Monkey's opening and Dragon's storage where Water energy runs at its strongest. Today favors Rat going at full strength rather than holding back - saying the whole plan instead of the safe summary, asking for what's actually wanted, showing up at real size in a meeting or a conversation that matters. Held-back effort costs more today than it saves.

3. Dragon - Today Is Literally Its Own Day

Dragon isn't just a member of today's trine - Dragon's branch is the day itself, and it sits at the storage point where the energy Monkey opened and Rat carried to its peak finally gets gathered in and kept. Paired with a nayin of "Covered Lamp Fire," that's a strong double signal: today rewards Dragon for protecting something rather than putting it on display - finishing a task quietly instead of announcing it, keeping a plan close instead of discussing it too early, guarding a flame that only needs shelter, not an audience, to keep burning.
