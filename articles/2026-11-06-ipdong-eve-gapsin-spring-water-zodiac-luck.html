November 6: Autumn's Last Day Before Ipdong Meets a Gapsin Day's Wood Cut Down Into Spring Water - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck

Published November 6, 2026

November 6, 2026 is autumn's last full day on Korea's traditional calendar - Ipdong (입동), the solar term that opens winter, doesn't officially land until tomorrow evening, Saturday, November 7, at 6:52 PM. Today itself runs on a Gapsin (甲申) day: Yang Wood sitting on a Monkey branch that's pure Metal, the stem-branch pairing old readings call Metal cutting straight into Wood. But underneath that clash sits a gentler layer - the day's classical nayin name is Cheonjungsu, 泉中水, 'spring water,' the quiet current old masters say keeps flowing even after the tree standing over it has been cut down. There's a second, dated detail worth noticing: Monkey is the exact opening point of the Monkey-Rat-Dragon Water Trine - meaning the same Water the calendar is about to officially release tomorrow is already breaking ground today, on the one day that carries its branch. Three zodiac animals are living that early opening.

1. Monkey - The Seed Planted a Day Early

It's the Monkey's own branch holding up today's sky, and it happens to sit on the single point where the Water Trine begins - a full day before winter makes it official on the calendar. Something quiet you've only just started (a saving habit, a first outreach message, an idea you haven't told anyone about) isn't supposed to show results yet, and today it doesn't need to. Luck favors planting now and trusting the water moving underground, rather than forcing a tree that's already been cut to keep growing above it.

2. Rat - The Current Already Running Full

The Rat sits at this trine's fullest stretch, the point where the Water Trine's current runs strongest - and on a day otherwise ruled by Metal cutting into Wood, that current keeps moving regardless of the clash above it. Momentum you built over the past few weeks is close to its peak; today rewards riding it rather than second-guessing it. A decision that's felt obvious for a while is safe to finally act on, before winter slows everything down tomorrow.

3. Dragon - The Vault Winter Is About to Close

The Dragon holds the Water Trine's storage point, the spot where whatever this cycle gathered gets sealed away right before Ipdong turns the calendar's page tomorrow. Paperwork, savings, a half-finished agreement with someone else - today is the last clean chance to close it properly while it's still technically autumn. What gets filed away whole today stays whole through the months ahead.
