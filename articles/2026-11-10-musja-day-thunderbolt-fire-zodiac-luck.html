November 10: A Musja Day — Calm Earth Floating on Water, With a Thunderbolt Hidden Underneath, and the 3 Zodiac Signs Catching the Spark

Published November 10, 2026

Tuesday, November 10 carries no national holiday and isn't even a traditional "son-eomneun-nal" moving day — on the calendar, it looks like the plainest Tuesday of the month. But in the saju calendar, today is a Musja (戊子) day: Yang Earth (戊) sitting directly on top of Water (子), the sign of the Rat. On the surface, that's firm, settled ground — like a pond frozen solid enough to stand on. The hidden element underneath this exact stem-branch pair, though, is one Koreans call "Byeokryeokhwa" (霹靂火), or Thunderbolt Fire: a sudden, cracking spark that fires off in an instant rather than building slowly. Calm ground with lightning underneath it — that paradox is shaping luck for three zodiac animals today.

1. Ox — The Quiet Bond Clicks Into Place

The Ox forms a direct six-harmony bond with today's Rat energy, and it shows up as a conversation or agreement that suddenly, almost effortlessly, falls into place. Something you've been circling around with a partner, roommate, or coworker gets settled today in far fewer words than you expected. Say the plain thing out loud — it lands better than you think.

2. Monkey — A Flash of Insight Worth Acting On

The Monkey shares today's water trine, and that Thunderbolt Fire underneath turns into one sharp, sudden idea — the kind that arrives fully formed rather than through grinding it out. Write it down the moment it hits, even if it seems too simple. The quick version of the idea is usually the right one today.

3. Dragon — Stillness That's Actually Building Charge

For the Dragon, also sharing today's water trine, a stretch that's felt quiet or stalled is really just gathering energy before a release. Don't mistake today's calm for nothing happening — small, patient moves made now are what the coming spark will run on.

Everyone Gets a Taste of the Spark

A Musja day isn't marked on any calendar, and the jolt underneath today's stillness isn't reserved for just these three signs. If something in your life has looked quiet and settled lately, today is a good day to notice the charge building underneath it — and to act the moment it actually sparks.
