September 29: The 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck for Korea's October Golden Week

Published September 29, 2026

It's the last Tuesday of September, which means October is just around the corner — and this year it's a special one. October 3 (Gaecheonjeol, Korea's National Foundation Day) falls on a Saturday, so the following Monday, October 5, becomes a substitute holiday. Add Hangul Day on October 9, and just three vacation days in between can stretch the whole run into a 9-day break. With plenty of people already planning trips, here are the three zodiac animals with the strongest travel and rest luck this season.

1. Tiger — The More You Move, the Better It Gets

The Tiger's naturally active energy gets an extra boost during this early-October stretch. Whether it's a trip abroad or somewhere domestic you've been meaning to visit, moving toward it now is likely to bring a better experience than expected. Book your stay and a rough itinerary early, and the whole holiday should flow smoothly.

2. Rabbit — Luck Doubles With Family and Close Friends

For the Rabbit, this golden week favors company over solo time. If it's been a while since you've gathered with family or close friends, be the one to suggest it. A short trip or even a nearby outing together does more than pass the time — it sets a good tone that carries into November.

3. Monkey — Unexpected Ideas From Unfamiliar Places

The Monkey tends to get an unusual spark from new places and first-time activities this holiday. Even just wandering into a cafe or neighborhood outside your usual routine can clear your head — and an idea that surfaces along the way could turn out genuinely useful once November's work picks back up.

Not One of These Three? No Need to Worry

A golden week doesn't mean everyone has to travel. For some zodiac animals, resting fully at home is exactly enough, and with the seasons changing, pacing yourself matters more than packing your schedule. Let how your body actually feels shape your plans, not the calendar.
