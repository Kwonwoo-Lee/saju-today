October 27: Korea's Film Day Meets a Gapsul Day's Buried Fire - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck

Published October 27, 2026

October 27 is 영화의 날 (Yeonghwa-ui Nal), Korea's Film Day - marking 107 years since the night in 1919 when The Righteous Revenge screened at Seoul's Dansungsa theater, a kinodrama that mixed a live stage play with filmed outdoor scenes. It's often cited as Korea's first film, though historians still argue a newsreel shown the same night deserves that title instead. Not a public holiday, just a quiet industry anniversary studios and cinemas still nod to every year. On the Saju calendar, today is also a Gapsul (甲戌) day: Yang Wood sitting on top of the Dog's Earth branch. The Dog looks like solid ground on the surface, but underneath it stores two hidden stems - Fire and Metal - banked like embers under packed earth, waiting for a spark. A fitting pairing for a day built around the industry of light. Three zodiac animals line up with the Dog today to form Saju's Tiger-Horse-Dog Fire Trine, and they're the ones most likely to feel that buried fire catch.

1. Tiger - Supplies the Spark

The Tiger opens today's Fire Trine, which puts it closest to whatever lights first. An idea you've been sitting on for weeks - a pitch, a side project, a plan you half-shelved - finds its moment to actually move today. Say it out loud to the right person before the day's half over; wait much longer and the spark cools again.

2. Horse - Keeps It Burning

The Horse holds the trine's middle point, the part that turns one spark into something with real heat. Momentum you build today is less likely to fizzle out tomorrow than usual - a project you pick back up now has genuine staying power. Push a little harder than normal on something you started and never finished; today it sticks.

3. Dog - Hosts the Fire

It's the Dog's own branch underfoot today, so the fire stored in that ground belongs to it most directly. Something you've been quietly building without telling many people - a skill, a side plan, a private project - is closer to ready than you think. Let a little of that work finally show today instead of keeping it buried any longer.
