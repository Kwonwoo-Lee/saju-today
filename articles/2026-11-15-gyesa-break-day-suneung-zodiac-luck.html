November 15: A Gyesa 'Break' Day Presses Water Over Hidden Fire, Four Days Before Suneung - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Cleanest Luck

Published November 15, 2026

Sunday, November 15 carries no national holiday or folk observance of its own - but it's the day Korea's test-takers start counting down to Thursday, when Suneung, the national college entrance exam that can shape a student's next decade, finally arrives. In the Saju calendar, today is a Gyesa (癸巳) day: Yin Water (癸) sitting directly on the Snake's branch (巳) - and the Snake isn't an empty perch. Its hidden stems carry a live Fire core, so today's chart is Water pressed right over a buried flame, pressure holding in a heat it can't fully put out. The sixty-day nayin table still calls the pair Jangnyu-su (長流水), Long-flowing Water, the same name it gave yesterday's Imjin day - but today closes that two-day name out; tomorrow's pair starts fresh. The old twelve-day-officer calendar adds its own verdict: today is a Pa (破), a Break day, built for ending and clearing rather than starting - which is exactly the mood four days out from a test that rewards releasing stale nerves, not cramming new ones in. Here are the three zodiac animals carrying that clearing energy today.

1. Snake - Breaking Open Its Own Ground

Snake isn't just today's branch - it's the exact point where the Metal trine (Snake-Rooster-Ox) begins, the opening move of a three-branch alliance rather than its storage point. On a Break day, that opening energy turns literal: whatever stale plan has been sitting half-finished is better demolished than patched today. Send the resignation, drop the plan that no longer fits, clear the ground - whatever you build after today's break will stand on cleaner footing than anything propped up on top of it.

2. Ox - The Trine's Quiet Reservoir

Ox closes out the same Metal trine that Snake opens, the point where that current settles and stores. A Break day doesn't threaten what's genuinely solid - it strips away whatever's been propped on top of it. Something you've been sitting on, a draft, a saved decision, a half-written plan, is ready to be cut down to its real size today; edit out the padding and what's left will actually hold.

3. Monkey - The Bond a Break Day Can't Touch

Monkey forms a direct six-harmony bond with today's Snake branch, the kind of tie that survives exactly the clearing a Break day brings. Noise gets cut away today - small talk, hedging, the maybes - and what's left exposed is whatever was real underneath. If a connection has felt a little unclear lately, today is less about adding more words to it and more about letting the extra ones fall away.
