September 30: The 3 Zodiac Signs With the Best Luck for Today's Big Autumn Chill

Published September 30, 2026

It's Wednesday, September 30 — the last day of September, and Korea is feeling it. Morning rain is clearing over the central regions today, but temperatures still swing wildly: afternoon highs near 24°C (75°F) drop to the mid-teens by evening, a gap of 10-15°C (18-27°F) in a single day. It's the kind of day-night lurch that catches sleep schedules and immune systems off guard right as October's colder air moves in. A few zodiac signs are riding out today's swing better than most.

1. Virgo — Steadiest on the Last Day of Its Own Season

Virgo season (Aug 23 – Sep 22) technically ended last week, but its practical, prepared energy is still running strong today. Packing a layer for the evening before you leave home, or rescheduling an outdoor plan around this morning's rain, comes naturally — and that small bit of planning keeps the whole day running smoothly while others scramble.

2. Capricorn — Barely Notices the Cold Snap

Capricorn's steady discipline makes today's temperature whiplash feel like a non-event. Routines you've already built — an early bedtime, a consistent meal schedule — carry you through the swing without much disruption. It's also a solid day to lock in a plan for October that others are still hesitating over.

3. Cancer — Turns the Chill Into Comfort

Cancer feels the shift in the air first, and today that sensitivity works in your favor. The instinct to head home a little earlier, brew something warm, or check in on someone who might be under the weather brings a genuinely cozy, good-mood evening — and maybe a warmer conversation with someone close to you than you expected.

Not One of Today's Three? That's Fine.

A 10-15°C swing catches everyone off guard occasionally, whatever your sign. A light jacket by the door and an earlier bedtime tonight go a long way, no matter where you land in the zodiac.
