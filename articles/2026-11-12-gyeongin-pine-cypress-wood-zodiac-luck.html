November 12: A Gyeongin Day's Metal Looks Set to Cut Wood, but Its Hidden Name Is Pine-and-Cypress Wood - 3 Zodiac Animals Rooting Into the Luck

Published November 12, 2026

Thursday, November 12 carries no national holiday or folk observance of its own — on the calendar, it's one of the plainest days left before winter sets in deeper. But in the Saju calendar, today is a Gyeongin (庚寅) day: Yang Metal (庚) sitting directly on a Tiger branch (寅), which plain five-element logic reads as Metal cutting straight into Wood — an axe laid right across a tree. The old almanacs name this exact stem-branch pair differently, though: Songbaek-mok (松柏木), Pine-and-Cypress Wood, the evergreen that keeps its color through frost rather than the timber that falls to the blade. Ten days after Ipdong marked winter's start, that reversal — wood that survives the axe instead of falling to it — is rooting itself into three zodiac animals today.

1. Pig - The Hidden Partner Striking Root

The Pig forms a direct six-harmony bond with today's Tiger branch, and that quiet partnership is what turns Metal's threat into Wood holding its ground. A connection you've kept half-formal - a mentor, an old collaborator, someone you've only exchanged a few polite messages with - is ready to root into something sturdier today. Reach out plainly; the bond is already there, it just needs one more exchange to take hold.

2. Rabbit - Wood at Its Fullest Green

The Rabbit sits at the peak of the same Wood current running under today's sky, the point where it grows fullest and greenest. Whatever's been quietly expanding in your life - a skill, a client list, a body of work - is ready to be shown at its current size today, not shrunk down out of modesty. Let people see how much you've actually grown.

3. Goat - Roots Going in for Winter

The Goat closes this Wood current into storage, the point where growth stops reaching outward and starts going down into roots instead. A plan you'd normally rush to finish benefits today from being left to settle instead - write it down, close the laptop, and let it set overnight. What you don't force today holds up better once the cold actually arrives.
