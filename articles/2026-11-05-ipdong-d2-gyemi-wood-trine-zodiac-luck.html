November 5: D-2 to Ipdong, Korea's 'Winter's Start,' Meets a Gyemi Day's Water Settling Into the Goat's Harvest Vault - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck

Published November 5, 2026

November 5, 2026 sits two days ahead of Ipdong (입동), the solar term that marks winter's official start on Korea's traditional calendar - it lands this Saturday, November 7, the point where the almanac has long told farmers to finish gathering the last of the harvest and seal their stores before the cold sets in. On the Saju calendar, today itself is a Gyemi (癸未) day: Yin Water, the kind old readings picture as quiet dew or groundwater rather than a river in flood, settling onto a Goat branch that is Earth at its most sealed - the exact point in the cycle where the Pig-Rabbit-Goat Wood Trine stores away a whole growing season's effort before winter can touch it. Two days out from a turn everyone will have to make, three zodiac animals are already living it.

1. Pig - The Seed Already Chosen for Next Spring

The Pig opens this Wood Trine from its starting edge, the quiet decision made before any sprout is visible - which effort gets carried into the next season and which gets left in this year's field. Today favors picking that one thing now, before winter forces the choice for you: the project, the relationship, the plan you already know deserves another year. Name it today while it's still your decision to make.

2. Rabbit - The Growth That's Gone as Far as It Will This Year

The Rabbit sits at this trine's fullest reach, the point where this year's growing is done growing - not stalled, just finished for the season. Something you've been pushing to get bigger or further has probably already arrived; today rewards admitting that and shifting from growing it to using it. Stop reaching for one more inch and start harvesting what's actually there.

3. Goat - Today's Own Vault, Sealing the Season In

It's the Goat's own branch under today's sky, sitting exactly at the Wood Trine's storage point - the vault where this year's work gets sealed in before anyone can unravel it over winter. Finish the paperwork, close the loop, write the summary you've been putting off: today is built for locking a finished thing down so it survives the quiet months untouched. What you seal properly now, you get back whole in spring.
