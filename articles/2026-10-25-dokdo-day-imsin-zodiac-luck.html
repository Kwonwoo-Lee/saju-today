October 25: Dokdo Day Meets an Imsin Water-Monkey Day - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck

Published October 25, 2026

October 25 is Dokdo Day in Korea, marking the anniversary of a 1900 imperial edict that formally listed the Dokdo islets - a small outcrop in the East Sea that Korea has administered since 1954 - as part of Ulleungdo county. It isn't an official public holiday, but this year it's drawing unusual attention: a bill in the National Assembly proposes to give the day formal legal standing, and a province-run 'Cyber Dokdo School' online festival is running through October 30 for students and the public alike. On the Saju side, today falls on Imsin (壬申) - a Yang Water day sitting on the Monkey branch, whose hidden Metal is quietly what generates that water underneath. It's a day for things that were building out of sight to finally surface. Today's branch also anchors one of Saju's classic three-way alliances, the Monkey-Rat-Dragon Water Trine, and three zodiac animals are riding that current hardest.

1. Monkey - Standing on Home Ground

Today's branch is the Monkey's own sign, so the Monkey gets a rare dose of quiet stability - less need to perform, more room to just function. A negotiation or conversation that's been stalling for days may loosen on its own, simply because you're walking in more relaxed than usual. Resist the urge to force an outcome; today it arrives on its own schedule.

2. Rat - The Water Trine's Quiet Partner

The Rat completes the Monkey-Rat-Dragon alliance from the water side, and that connection pulls things toward the Rat from a distance today - a message from someone you haven't heard from in a while, information arriving right when you need it, a plan someone else set in motion finally reaching you. Stay reachable rather than chasing; what's coming is already on its way.

3. Dragon - What Was Underground Surfaces

As the third point of today's water trine, the Dragon gets the clearest version of today's theme: something prepared quietly over weeks is finally ready to be shown. If you've been sitting on an idea, a proposal, or a piece of news, today is unusually well-suited to finally putting it out into the open rather than holding it back one more day.
