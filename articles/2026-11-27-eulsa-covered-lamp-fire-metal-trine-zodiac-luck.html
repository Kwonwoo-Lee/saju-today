November 27: Eulsa Day Completes 'Covered Lamp Fire' While the Snake Quietly Opens a Brand New Trine - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck Today

Published November 27, 2026

Friday, November 27 is an Eulsa (乙巳) day in the sixty-day cycle - Yin Wood (乙) sitting directly on the Snake's own branch (巳). That pairing matters more than it looks: Snake is itself a Fire branch, so today's Wood sits right on top of Fire instead of Earth, and feeds it the way a wick feeds a flame - no sheltering required, just a steady, natural supply. That's a quiet contrast with yesterday's Gapjin day, where Yang Wood sat on the Dragon's Earth and needed cover just to keep its small flame alive. In the nayin table, today actually completes the same pairing Gapjin opened: Bokdeung-hwa (覆燈火), Covered Lamp Fire, closes out today rather than starting fresh. But there's a second story running underneath that has nothing to do with yesterday at all - the Snake branch is also the opening point of its own trine, Snake-Rooster-Ox (巳酉丑), the Metal trine, a completely new three-sign cycle just getting underway. Here are the three zodiac animals feeling today's layered energy most directly.

1. Snake - Today Is Your Day, Twice Over

When the day's branch matches your own sign, the day already speaks your language - and today it's saying two things at once. Snake closes out a two-day nayin story while simultaneously opening a brand new trine, which makes today less about picking one mode and more about holding both: finishing something quietly while a separate, newer thread starts to stir. For Snake, that could mean wrapping up a task that's been dragging on while, almost unnoticed, planting the first seed of whatever comes next.

2. Rooster - Where the New Trine Will Eventually Peak

Rooster is the flourishing point of the same Snake-Rooster-Ox trine that opens today - the spot where this new cycle's energy will eventually burn brightest, long before it actually gets there. For Rooster, today isn't the day to expect a payoff yet, but it is a good day to notice which conversation or opportunity just quietly started, because it's the one worth returning to later. Patience now sets up the better timing ahead.

3. Ox - The Far End of a Cycle That Just Began

Ox sits at the storage point of the same trine - the place where all of this cycle's energy eventually settles and gets put away, months down the line. For Ox, today's opening elsewhere doesn't ask for any action at all; it's simply a reminder that something distant is now in motion. The more useful move today is tending to whatever is already stored and settled, rather than reaching for something new.
