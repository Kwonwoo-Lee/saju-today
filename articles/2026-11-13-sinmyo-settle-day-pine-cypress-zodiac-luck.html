November 13: A Sinmyo 'Settle' Day Carries Yesterday's Wood Into a Finer Blade - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck

Published November 13, 2026

Friday, November 13 carries no national holiday or folk observance of its own — on the calendar, it's one of the quieter Fridays of the month. But in the Saju calendar, today is a Sinmyo (辛卯) day: Yin Metal (辛) sitting directly on a Rabbit branch (卯) — and under the sixty-day nayin table, it shares its exact wood name with yesterday's Gyeongin day: Songbaek-mok (松柏木), Pine-and-Cypress Wood, the evergreen that holds its color through frost. Yesterday that wood faced Yang Metal, an axe laid straight across the trunk. Today the blade turns finer — Yin Metal reads less like an axe and more like a chisel or a hairpin, shaping the wood rather than felling it, and it's sitting right on the Rabbit's own branch instead of just visiting it. The old twelve-day-officer calendar adds one more layer: today is a Jeong (定), a Settle day, the one built for signing, finalizing, and anything meant to last. Here are the three zodiac animals carrying that settled luck today.

1. Rabbit - Living Its Own Day Twice Over

The Rabbit isn't just represented by today's branch - it IS today's branch, doubled onto itself. That's rare enough to matter: whatever you'd normally soften or hand off to someone else today, do yourself instead. A conversation you'd normally let a colleague run, a decision you'd usually defer - take it directly. The day is already built around you; stop acting like a guest in it.

2. Dog - The Six-Harmony Partner Locking It In

The Dog forms a direct six-harmony bond with today's Rabbit branch, and that bond is exactly what a Settle day rewards. A verbal agreement that's been hanging without paperwork, a half-promised collaboration, a plan you've only discussed out loud - today is the day to actually write it down and make it official. The goodwill is already there; today just asks you to put it on record.

3. Goat - Where This Wood Current Goes to Rest

The Goat closes the same Wood trine that the Rabbit sits at the peak of - the point where growth stops reaching outward and settles into its final shape. Fitting, for a Settle day: a project you've been tempted to keep expanding benefits more today from being closed out than added to. Pick a stopping point and call it finished - today rewards the close, not the continuation.
