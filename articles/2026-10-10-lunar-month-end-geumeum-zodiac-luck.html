October 10: The 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck on Geumeum, the Lunar Month's Last Quiet Day

Published October 10, 2026

After nine days built almost entirely around Hangul Day and the holidays before it, Saturday, October 10 doesn't look like much on paper. No holiday, no extra day off, nothing marked in red. But on the lunar calendar it's geumeum (그믐) - the last day of the eighth lunar month, the quiet stretch right before the new moon rolls everything over into the ninth month on Sunday. Traditionally it's treated as a settling day: old business gets wrapped up, small debts get cleared, and nothing new really gets started until the fresh month arrives. A few zodiac animals are reading that low-key timing better than most today.

1. Monkey - Uses the Lull to Try Something Small

The Monkey treats a quiet Saturday like this as rare free room rather than dead time. With no holiday plans pulling at the calendar and the new lunar month not here yet, it's a good window to test a small idea nobody would have time to notice on a busier day - a different route, a new recipe, a message to someone unexpected. Nothing about today demands a big move, and that's exactly what lets the Monkey's instinct for playful experiments pay off.

2. Snake - Notices What the Holidays Actually Changed

Everyone else is still catching up on sleep from the Hangul Day stretch, but the Snake is quietly taking stock of what shifted underneath it - a friendship that got closer over a holiday meal, a habit that slipped and hasn't come back, a plan that quietly died while everyone was off. That kind of unglamorous noticing rarely gets credit in the moment, but today it gives the Snake a clear read nobody else in the room has yet.

3. Goat - Clears the Small Stuff Before the New Moon

The Goat has a practical sense that geumeum is for finishing, not starting, and leans into it today. An unanswered message, a small favor still owed, a bill or chore that's been pushed to "later" for weeks - clearing it now, before Sunday's new lunar month opens a clean page, costs almost nothing. Leave it for next week and it just gets heavier.

Not One of Today's Three? That's Fine.

Geumeum doesn't care who's paying attention - the lunar month ends the same way for everyone, whether you track it or not. These three animals just happen to have good instincts for a day that rewards quietly finishing things over starting them.
