October 11: The 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck as the New Moon Opens the Ninth Lunar Month

Published October 11, 2026

Yesterday was geumeum, the hushed last day of the eighth lunar month. Today, Sunday, October 11, is its mirror image: sakil (삭일), the new moon that rolls the calendar over into the ninth lunar month. Traditionally it's treated less as a day for big moves and more as a blank page - a good moment to set a direction for the weeks ahead, even if the real action waits. What makes today a little unusual is the overlap: this fresh lunar month opens on the exact same day the weekend runs out, so a brand-new month and a brand-new workweek both start within about twelve hours of each other. A few zodiac animals are reading that doubled-up fresh-start energy better than the rest today.

1. Rabbit - Sets a Direction Without Forcing a Decision

The Rabbit's instinct is to feel out a new month before committing to it, and that patience fits a new-moon day perfectly. Rather than locking in a big plan on day one, the Rabbit jots down what it actually wants from the next four weeks - a habit, a conversation, a goal left half-finished last month - and lets the details sort themselves out later. On a day built for planting intentions rather than harvesting results, that restraint is exactly what pays off.

2. Dog - Turns a Quiet Sunday Into Real Monday Prep

Everyone says they'll "get ready for the week" on Sunday, but the Dog actually does it: a real schedule check, a message sent tonight instead of a scramble tomorrow morning, a bag packed before bed. With the new lunar month opening on top of the old week closing, there's unusually good reason to want Monday to start clean, and the Dog's practical, loyal streak makes sure it actually does.

3. Pig - Opens the Month With One Generous Move

Folk tradition treats the new moon as a day worth opening warmly rather than cautiously, and the Pig leans into that without overthinking it - a kind message to someone overdue for one, a small treat shared, a little generosity that costs almost nothing today. How a lunar month opens is said to color how it goes, and the Pig's instinct to start with warmth rather than caution gives it an edge right as the ninth month begins.

Not One of Today's Three? That's Fine.

The new moon doesn't check who's paying attention - the ninth lunar month opens the same way for everyone, whether you track it or not. These three animals just happen to have good instincts for a day that rewards setting a direction over forcing a result.
