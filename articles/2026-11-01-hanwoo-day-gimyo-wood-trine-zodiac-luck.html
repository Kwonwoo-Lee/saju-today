November 1: Korea's Hanwoo Day Meets a Gimyo Day's Sprouting Farmland - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck

Published November 1, 2026

November 1 is Hanwoo Day in Korea - the beef industry's own promotional holiday, picked for a small bit of wordplay: stack the hanja for ox, 牛, three times and the strokes trace out three number '1's, matching the date 11/1. Butchers and restaurants run beef specials around it all week. On the Saju calendar, today is a Gimyo (己卯) day: Yin Earth, the kind the old readings describe as tilled farmland soil that embraces and feeds whatever grows in it, with a Wood Rabbit branch sprouting straight up out of that ground. It completes the Pig-Rabbit-Goat Wood Trine, and three zodiac animals are the ones sprouting fastest today.

1. Rabbit - The Sprout Itself

It's the Rabbit's own branch holding up today's sky, so whatever's pushing up through your plans right now is growing on ground that's entirely yours. A new idea, a side project, or a request you've been sitting on is ready to break the surface. Say it out loud today instead of waiting for more certainty - the soil underneath it is already fertile.

2. Pig - Where the Furrow Was Already Turned

The Pig opens the Wood Trine, the row a farmer tills first before anything else gets planted. Groundwork you laid weeks ago - a favor extended, a plan quietly set in motion - starts paying off today, earlier than you expected. Someone circles back with good news; take it as confirmation the timing was right, not luck.

3. Goat - The Harvest That Closes the Row

The Goat closes the trine at the far end of the field, where whatever grew gets gathered in. Today favors finishing over starting - wrapping up a deal, a document, a conversation that's been dragging. Don't let a good result sit half-collected; bring it home while the Rabbit's growth is still fresh.
