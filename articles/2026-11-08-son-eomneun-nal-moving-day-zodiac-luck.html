November 8: It's a 'Son-Eomneun-Nal' — Korea's Spirit-Free Moving Day, and the 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck Today

Published November 8, 2026

Sunday, November 8 isn't just a day off this week. On the Korean lunar calendar it lands on the 29th of the ninth month, and since the date ends in a 9, it's one of this month's "son-eomneun-nal" (손없는 날) — days when the mischievous spirit called "son," believed to roam the four directions looking for trouble, is said to take a break. For centuries, Koreans have timed moves, lease signings, weddings, and store openings around exactly these dates, and moving companies still see bookings spike whenever one falls on a weekend. There are only five of them this November, and today is one. Three zodiac animals are getting an extra push.

1. Ox — The Weight Finally Settles

The Ox has been carrying something heavy for weeks — an unmade decision, a half-packed box, a plan not yet announced. Today it finally lands in place without a fight. If you're moving, signing paperwork, or just making a space feel like yours, do it today; whatever you set down now tends to stay put.

2. Snake — Reading the Exact Moment to Move

The Snake reads timing better than most signs, and today that instinct is especially sharp. A listing, an opening, or an opportunity that's been sitting untouched suddenly looks worth a closer look. Trust the pull to act now instead of waiting for a "better" day — today is that day.

3. Pig — Building the New Nest

For the Pig, today's luck is about comfort and foundations. Rearranging furniture, finally starting a new routine, organizing a space the way you actually want to live in it — small domestic wins stick today. Don't skip the little details; they're what make a fresh start feel like home.

Not Moving Today? It Still Counts

A son-eomneun-nal isn't only for moving trucks. Any fresh start you've been putting off — a new habit, a cleared-out drawer, a conversation you've been avoiding — tends to go unusually smoothly on a day like this, whichever zodiac animal you are.
