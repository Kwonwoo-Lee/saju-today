November 23: A Sinchuk Day Closes Out 'Wall Top Earth' at the Metal Trine's Storage Point - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck Today

Published November 23, 2026

Monday, November 23 is the first full workday since Minor Snow (小雪) landed yesterday afternoon, and the cold it brought is settling in for good. Today is also a Sinchuk (辛丑) day, Yin Metal resting on the Ox branch - and because yesterday's Gyeongja day opened a brand-new nayin pair, today's Sinchuk closes it out: Byeoksangto (壁上土), Wall Top Earth, the plaster that was only just applied yesterday and has now had a full day to set against the wall. There's a second layer to it, too - today's own Ox branch happens to be the storage point of the Snake-Rooster-Ox Metal trine, one of saju's three great samhap groupings, where Snake quietly starts the current and Rooster carries it to full flourish before Ox banks it for good. Here are the three zodiac animals reading today's newly set earth and gathering metal energy best.

1. Ox - Today Mirrors You, Right as You Close the Books

When the day's own branch matches your sign, the day already reflects you - and today that reflection lands on the exact day the Metal trine gets stored away, with Ox itself as the vault. For Ox, that makes today good for finishing rather than starting - wrapping up a task that's been dragging, settling a number, filing away something that's been sitting half-done. What gets closed out today tends to stay closed.

2. Snake - Where This Same Current First Starts Moving

Snake sits at the generation point of the same trine, the place where the Metal current first stirs before it reaches Rooster's full flourish or Ox's final storage. For Snake, today favors opening something new rather than finishing it - a first draft, a first outreach, a quiet beginning that won't show results for a while yet, much like metal still being refined before it's ready for anything.

3. Rooster - The Trine's Flourishing Peak, on a Day the Earth Just Finished Setting

Rooster is the flourishing point of the same trine, where the Metal current runs at its strongest rather than just starting or settling - and that peak lands on a day when the ground underneath has just finished hardening. For Rooster, today favors pushing something to its sharpest, most finished form while the footing underneath is freshly solid - polishing a pitch, finalizing a plan, putting real force behind something you've already set up.
