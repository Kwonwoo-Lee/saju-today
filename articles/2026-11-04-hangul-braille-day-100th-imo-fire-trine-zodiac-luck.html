November 4: Korean Braille Turns 100 on an Imo Day's River Water Over Noon Fire - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck

Published November 4, 2026

November 4, 2026 marks exactly 100 years since Park Du-seong (박두성) unveiled Hunmaengjeongeum (훈맹정음) - literally "the correct sound that teaches the blind" - Korea's own Hangul Braille. He'd spent six years quietly testing raised-dot patterns with his students at a Seoul school for the blind, refusing to let them keep reading and writing in borrowed Japanese braille, until the system he finished in 1926 could render every Hangul consonant, vowel, and number in just six dots. Korea made the date a legal Hangul Braille Day in 2021, and its hundredth lands on an Imo (壬午) day on the Saju calendar: Yang Water, the kind the old readings picture as a deep river or an open sea, sitting directly on a Horse branch that is Fire at its most blinding, noontime peak. Patient water pressed right against the brightest flame - it opens the Tiger-Horse-Dog Fire Trine, and three zodiac animals are carrying that friction into real luck today.

1. Tiger - The Spark Before Anyone Was Watching

The Tiger opens this Fire Trine the way one stubborn idea opens six years of quiet testing - before any dot pattern had proven itself to a single reader. Start the thing nobody's asked to see yet. A draft, a prototype, a first conversation you've been putting off because it isn't ready - begin it today anyway. What looks unfinished now is exactly how every lasting system begins.

2. Horse - Today's Own Flame, at Its Peak

It's the Horse's own branch under today's sky, sitting right at the Fire Trine's peak - the point where quiet work finally gets seen in full daylight. Something you've kept low-key is ready to stand in the open today: a finished piece, a result you can finally show, a truth you've stopped softening. Let it be seen at full brightness; today isn't the day to dim it for anyone's comfort.

3. Dog - Where the Fire Banks Into Something Lasting

The Dog closes the trine, the point where today's fire settles into embers that keep burning quietly without anyone tending them. A system, a habit, or an agreement you set up some time ago is proving today that it holds on its own - it doesn't need you watching it every hour anymore. Trust what you built to keep working while you look elsewhere.
