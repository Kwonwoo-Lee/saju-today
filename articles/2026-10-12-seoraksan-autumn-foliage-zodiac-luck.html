October 12: The 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck as Seoraksan's Autumn Colors Start Spreading Down the Mountain

Published October 12, 2026

It's Monday, October 12, and Korea's autumn-foliage season is officially underway. Seoraksan - the jagged, granite-peaked national park on the east coast that is Korea's unofficial starting line for fall colors every year - turned at its summit in late September, right on schedule, and forest authorities are now forecasting peak color there around October 20, spreading south and down in elevation through the rest of the month. For a lot of people, this week is when the trip-planning group chats start buzzing. Three zodiac animals are especially well-positioned to catch the good timing.

1. Rabbit - Quick to Book, Quick to Enjoy

The Rabbit moves fast once a plan feels right, and today that instinct pays off. Whoever checks the weekend weather and reserves a cabin, a bus ticket, or a trailhead parking spot first usually gets the better option before everyone else piles on. If a hiking trip or a short getaway has been sitting half-planned in your notes app, this is the day to lock in a date rather than keep comparing options.

2. Horse - Energy Made for Moving Outdoors

The Horse's natural restlessness turns into a real advantage this week. Sitting still at a desk feels harder than usual, but that same energy is exactly what a mountain trail or a long outdoor walk calls for. Even a short hike after work or a weekend drive toward the colors can shake loose a mental block that's been sitting unsolved for days.

3. Rooster - An Eye for Exactly When the View Peaks

The Rooster has a sharp sense of timing and color, and this week that sense is unusually reliable. Trust your own read on when a trail will be at its best rather than only going by someone else's recommendation - you're likely to pick the right day, and maybe even the right route, before the crowds figure it out too.

Didn't Make Today's Three?

No need to read anything into it if your sign isn't above. A zodiac-animal forecast only looks at your birth year, which is a wide net - plenty of good days for every sign don't happen to line up with the week's biggest seasonal story. There's still plenty of ordinary good fortune to go around today.
