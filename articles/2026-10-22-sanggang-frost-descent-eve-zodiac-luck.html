October 22: The Eve of Sanggang, Korea's 'Frost's Descent' - 3 Zodiac Animals Feeling the Season's Last Turn

Published October 22, 2026

Thursday, October 22 is the last full day of true autumn on Korea's 24-solar-term calendar. Tomorrow evening, the sun crosses into Sanggang (상강, 霜降) - "Frost's Descent," the term sitting between Hanro (한로, Cold Dew) and Ipdong (입동, the Start of Winter) that traditionally marks the year's first hard frost across most of the country. It's also the unofficial starting gun for kimjang (김장), the household ritual of preparing a winter's worth of kimchi in one coordinated push, and an old saying holds that a heavy frost at Sanggang promises a good harvest the following year. Behind the seasons runs the five-element calendar, where autumn belongs to Metal and winter to Water - and today is the exact hinge where Metal's grip finally loosens and Water begins to rise. Three zodiac animals are positioned to feel that turn first, each for a different reason.

1. Rat - Already Living in Tomorrow's Element

The Rat's home element is Water, exactly what Metal is about to generate as the season turns, so the Rat tends to sense Sanggang's chill before the calendar actually confirms it. In practice that shows up as an early instinct: wrapping something up before a deadline tightens, or quietly setting aside supplies - much like a kimjang pantry filling early - before anyone else feels the need to. The Rat should trust that early read today rather than waiting for confirmation.

2. Rabbit - Through the Season's Last Squeeze Today

In the five-element control cycle, Metal overpowers Wood directly, and the Rabbit carries Wood as its own element - which has made this stretch of autumn feel like quiet pressure for the Rabbit, more managed than managing. Today is that pressure's last full day, because once Sanggang lands tomorrow, Water starts flowing in, and Water is exactly what feeds Wood. Something the Rabbit has been holding back on all week - a request, a plan, a word left unsaid - gets noticeably easier to act on by tomorrow, and today is the day to get ready for that shift rather than fight through one more round of the old pressure.

3. Goat - Built to Hold the Incoming Water Steady

The Goat's home element is Earth, and in the control cycle, Earth is exactly what contains and channels Water rather than being swept along by it - like a riverbank shaping water instead of resisting it. That makes the Goat the calmest presence today as everyone else braces for the cold: a role organizing supplies, setting a schedule, or simply keeping a household's kimjang preparations on track suits the Goat especially well right now. Other people's urgency about the coming frost is best met with steadiness, not matching speed.

Not One of Today's Three? That's Fine.

A seasonal hinge like this one - the year's first real frost arriving right as kimjang season opens - rewards almost anyone leaning toward steady, practical preparation today, not just these three signs. If you're not one of them, getting a head start on something winter will otherwise rush is still a solid bet.
