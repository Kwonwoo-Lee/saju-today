November 14: An Imjin Day Trades Two Days of Pine-and-Cypress Wood for Long-Flowing Water, on a 'Hold' Day Built for Grasping - 3 Zodiac Animals With the Best Luck

Published November 14, 2026

Saturday, November 14 carries no national holiday or folk observance of its own — on the calendar, it's a quiet start to the weekend. But in the Saju calendar, today is an Imjin (壬辰) day: Yang Water (壬) sitting directly on a Dragon branch (辰) — and the Dragon isn't a random perch for Water. In the old trine theory, 申子辰 (Monkey-Rat-Dragon) forms the Water bureau, and Dragon is its closing point, the reservoir where that whole current comes home to settle. Yet the sixty-day nayin table still names today's stem-branch pair Jangnyu-su (長流水), Long-flowing Water — not a still pond, but a river that keeps moving even after it reaches its own basin. The old twelve-day-officer calendar adds one more layer: today is a Jip (執), a Hold day, the one built for grasping and securing whatever is already in motion, rather than starting something new from a standing stop. Here are the three zodiac animals holding onto that current today.

1. Dragon - Sitting at Its Own Reservoir

The Dragon isn't just today's branch - it IS today's branch, the exact reservoir the day's Water current comes home to. That doubling is rare enough to matter: something you've been quietly accumulating - savings, goodwill, a body of work - has reached the point where it's actually yours to draw on, not just watch grow. Stop treating it like it still belongs to the future; use some of it today.

2. Rooster - The Six-Harmony Catch

The Rooster forms a direct six-harmony bond with today's Dragon branch, and a Hold day is built exactly for locking that kind of bond into place. A connection that's been drifting in good-but-undefined territory - replies without a plan, warmth without a next step - is ready to be caught and named today. Send the message that actually proposes something; the goodwill won't hold its shape much longer on its own.

3. Rat - Where the Current Runs Strongest

The Rat sits at the peak of the same Water trine that Dragon closes, the point where this current runs fastest and fullest. Whatever's been gathering speed in your life lately - a project, a reputation, an opportunity circling back - is easiest to grab onto exactly where it's moving fastest, today. Don't wait for it to slow down to a stop before you reach for it.
