What is Saju (BaZi)?

Saju Today calculates your real Four Pillars chart from true solar-term astronomy, then reads today's fortune, this year's outlook, and your compatibility with someone else against it — completely free, no signup.

What is Saju (BaZi)?

Saju, also known as BaZi or the Four Pillars of Destiny, is a traditional East Asian chart built from your exact birth year, month, day and hour. Each pillar pairs a Heavenly Stem with an Earthly Branch, and the balance between them is read against the Five Elements.

How accurate is this BaZi calculator?

Saju Today applies true solar time correction for your birthplace and adjusts for daylight saving, the same correction traditional practitioners use by hand, instead of just looking up an average calendar date.

What do Today's Fortune and This Year's Fortune show?

We compare the current day or year against your personal Day Master and its Ten God relationship, then break the reading into wealth, love and health.

How does the compatibility score work?

Enter both birth dates and we compare the Ten God relationship between the two Day Masters to produce a compatibility score from 0 to 100, along with a full written reading.

Is Saju Today free, and is my birth data stored?

Yes, it's completely free with no signup. Your chart is calculated in your browser each time you submit the form; we don't store your birth details on a server.
